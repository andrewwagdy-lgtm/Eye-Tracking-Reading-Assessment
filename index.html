Before we start

This task uses your webcam to estimate where on the screen you're looking while you read. Tracking runs entirely in your browser: no video is recorded or sent anywhere — only gaze coordinates and timestamps are saved, and only after you answer each question.

Calibration (about 20 seconds)

A dot will move around a box the same size as the reading passage. Just keep your eyes on the dot as it moves — no mouse, no clicking, hands off entirely. This teaches the system to map your gaze to screen position using exactly where you'll be reading.
