Beyond Correct Answers

Gaze-based reading process assessment — pilot build

Before we start

This task uses your webcam to estimate where on the screen you're looking while you read. Tracking runs entirely in your browser: no video is recorded or sent anywhere — only gaze coordinates and timestamps are saved, and only after you answer each question.

Calibration (about 1 minute)

13 dots will appear one at a time, each for 5 seconds. Just look steadily at each dot — no clicking needed. This teaches the system to map your gaze to screen position for this seat and lighting.

Accuracy check

A dot will appear at five spots around the screen, one at a time. Look steadily at each one without clicking — this checks accuracy across the whole screen, not just the center.
